Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 18, 2026

EcoQuery ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Chrome extension. Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not use the extension.

1. Information We Collect

EcoQuery collects and processes the following types of information, strictly for the purpose of providing its core functionality:

  • Usage Metrics: Number of ChatGPT queries submitted per day, estimated token counts, and calculated environmental impact metrics (CO₂ emissions, energy consumption in watt-hours, and water usage in milliliters).
  • User Preferences: Your selected daily usage goal (Easy, Medium, Hard, or Custom), notification settings, and threshold preferences.
  • Account Information (Optional): If you choose to create an account and sign in, we collect your email address and a unique user identifier to enable cross-device synchronization of your metrics and preferences.
🔒 Important Note: EcoQuery does NOT store, retain, or transmit the actual content of your ChatGPT conversations. Only numerical metrics (token counts and derived environmental calculations) are processed and stored.

2. How We Use Your Information

All information collected is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Calculating and displaying your real-time environmental impact metrics (CO₂, energy, water)
  • Tracking your progress toward daily usage goals
  • Saving your preferences and settings across browser sessions
  • Providing optional notifications when approaching usage limits
  • Enabling cross-device synchronization (only if you choose to sign in)
  • Improving the accuracy of our environmental impact estimations

3. Data Storage and Security

Local Storage: Usage metrics and preferences are stored locally on your device using Chrome's storage API. This data remains on your device and is not accessible to other applications.

Cloud Storage (Optional): If you choose to create an account and sign in, your data is securely stored in Firebase Firestore, a Google Cloud service. All data transmission occurs over HTTPS/TLS 1.3 encryption. Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256.

Security Measures: We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Data is only shared in the following limited circumstances:

  • Firebase (Google Cloud): When you choose to sign in, your data is stored with Firebase to enable synchronization. Firebase's privacy practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal requests.
  • Protection of Rights: We may disclose information to protect the rights, property, or safety of EcoQuery, our users, or others.

5. Permissions We Request

EcoQuery requests the following Chrome permissions, each serving a specific and necessary purpose:

  • storage: To save your usage metrics, goal preferences, and settings locally on your device.
  • alarms: To schedule daily resets of metrics at midnight and trigger optional daily summary notifications.
  • notifications: To alert you when approaching your daily usage limits (only if you enable this feature).
  • host_permissions (chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com): To detect your ChatGPT interactions and estimate the environmental impact of each query. The extension only operates on these specific domains.

6. Limited Use Policy Compliance

EcoQuery's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • We limit our use of user data to providing and improving EcoQuery's single purpose of environmental impact tracking.
  • We only transfer user data to third parties when necessary to provide or improve our single purpose, to comply with applicable laws, or as part of a merger or acquisition with explicit user consent.
  • We do not allow humans to read user data except in limited circumstances (with user consent, for security purposes, or to comply with laws).
  • We prohibit all other transfers, uses, or sales of user data, including for advertising, credit determination, or lending purposes.

7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access and Portability: You can view your usage metrics at any time through the extension popup.
  • Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated cloud data by contacting us. Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data immediately.
  • Opt-Out: You can disable notifications at any time through the extension's settings.
  • Do Not Track: EcoQuery does not respond to Do Not Track signals as we do not track users across websites.

8. Data Retention

Local Data: All locally stored data is automatically deleted when you uninstall the extension.

Cloud Data (Account Users): Your data is retained as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, all associated data is permanently deleted from Firebase within 30 days.

Inactive Accounts: Accounts that have been inactive for over 24 months may be deleted, and associated data will be permanently removed.

9. Children's Privacy

EcoQuery is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us, and we will delete such information.

10. International Data Transfers

If you choose to sign in and sync your data, your information may be transferred to and stored on servers located in the United States or other countries where Google operates data centers. By using the sync feature, you consent to such transfers.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date and posting the new policy. Your continued use of EcoQuery after such changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
