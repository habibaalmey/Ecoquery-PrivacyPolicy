Getting Started

EcoQuery tracks the environmental cost of your ChatGPT usage, estimating CO2 emissions, energy consumption, and water usage for every query you send. Here is how to get set up.

Installation

  1. Install EcoQuery from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Click the EcoQuery icon in your Chrome toolbar to open the popup.
  3. Set your daily usage goal (Easy, Medium, Hard, or Custom) in the Settings tab.
  4. Start using ChatGPT. EcoQuery will automatically begin tracking your queries.

Setting a Daily Goal

Open the extension popup and navigate to Settings. Choose a preset goal or enter a custom number of daily queries. EcoQuery will notify you when you approach your limit if notifications are enabled.

Optional Account Sign-In

EcoQuery works fully without an account. If you use ChatGPT across multiple devices, signing in allows your metrics and preferences to sync automatically. Account creation requires only an email address.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does EcoQuery calculate environmental impact?

EcoQuery estimates the number of tokens in each ChatGPT query and response, then applies published research-based coefficients to convert those token counts into CO2 emissions (grams), energy consumption (watt-hours), and water usage (millilitres). These are estimates, not exact measurements, as the precise infrastructure of ChatGPT is not publicly disclosed.

Does EcoQuery read my ChatGPT messages?

No. EcoQuery does not read, store, or transmit the content of your ChatGPT conversations. It only detects that a query was submitted and estimates the number of tokens based on interaction signals. The text of your messages is never accessed by the extension.

Why does the extension only work on ChatGPT?

EcoQuery is purposely scoped to chatgpt.com and chat.openai.com only. It does not operate on any other website. This is intentional and is reflected in the extension host permissions, which are limited to these two domains.

Do I need an account to use EcoQuery?

No. All core functionality including tracking, goals, and notifications works without an account. Your data is stored locally on your device. An account is only needed if you want to sync your metrics across multiple devices.

Does EcoQuery reset my data daily?

Yes. Daily usage metrics including query count, CO2, energy, and water totals reset automatically at midnight local time.

How do I turn off notifications?

Open the EcoQuery popup, go to Settings, and toggle off the notifications option. You can re-enable them at any time. Disabling notifications does not affect metric tracking.

Will EcoQuery slow down my browser or ChatGPT?

No. EcoQuery is lightweight and only performs simple numerical calculations. It does not inject large scripts, modify the ChatGPT interface, or perform background network operations during your conversations.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling the extension immediately and permanently removes all locally stored data from your device. If you have a signed-in account, contact us at ecoqueryofficial@gmail.com to request deletion of your cloud data. We will complete this within 30 days.

Troubleshooting

EcoQuery is not tracking my queries

  1. Confirm you are using ChatGPT at chatgpt.com or chat.openai.com. EcoQuery does not operate on other domains.
  2. Check that the extension is enabled in Chrome by going to chrome://extensions and confirming EcoQuery is toggled on.
  3. Try refreshing the ChatGPT tab after installing or updating the extension.
  4. Ensure no other extensions are blocking content scripts on chatgpt.com.

The popup shows zero metrics

Metrics reset at midnight. If you have not yet submitted a query today the counter will show zero. Send a message in ChatGPT and return to the popup. Your first query should appear within a few seconds.

Notifications are not appearing

  1. Check that notifications are enabled in the EcoQuery Settings tab.
  2. Confirm Chrome has permission to show notifications by going to Chrome Settings, then Privacy and security, then Site settings, then Notifications.
  3. Check your operating system notification settings to ensure Chrome is not silenced.

The extension was removed or disabled automatically

Chrome may occasionally disable extensions if it detects an update conflict or policy change. Reinstall EcoQuery from the Chrome Web Store. If the problem persists, please contact us with your Chrome version and a description of the issue.

Still having issues? Contact us at ecoqueryofficial@gmail.com and include your Chrome version (found at chrome://settings/help) and a description of the problem.
