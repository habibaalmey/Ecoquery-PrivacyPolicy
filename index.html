Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 18, 2026  ·  Applies to EcoQuery Chrome Extension v1.3.0 and later

Key commitment: EcoQuery does not collect, store, or transmit the content of your ChatGPT conversations. Only anonymised numerical metrics -estimated token counts and derived environmental calculations -are ever processed or retained.

EcoQuery we are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your information transparently and responsibly. This Privacy Policy describes what data the EcoQuery Chrome Extension (the "Extension") collects, why it collects it, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

By installing or using the Extension, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy. If you do not agree with these practices, please do not install or use the Extension.

1. Information We Collect

EcoQuery collects only the minimum data necessary to provide its functionality. No conversation content is ever accessed or stored.

Usage Metrics

The number of ChatGPT queries submitted per day, estimated token counts, and derived environmental impact figures, including estimated CO₂ emissions (grams), energy consumption (watt-hours), and water usage (millilitres).

User Preferences

Your selected daily usage goal (Easy, Medium, Hard, or Custom), notification settings, and configured alert thresholds.

Account Information (Optional)

If you choose to create an account to enable cross-device synchronisation, we collect your email address and a unique user identifier. Account creation is entirely optional and is not required to use the Extension.

What we do not collect: EcoQuery does not access, read, store, or transmit the text content of your ChatGPT conversations, your browsing history on any other website, or any financial or health information.

2. Use of Information

All information collected is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Calculating and displaying real-time environmental impact estimates (CO₂, energy, water) within the Extension interface.
  • Tracking your progress toward self-defined daily usage goals.
  • Persisting your preferences and settings across browser sessions using Chrome's local storage API.
  • Delivering optional notifications when you approach or exceed your configured usage threshold.
  • Enabling cross-device synchronisation of metrics and preferences when signed in to an account.
  • Improving the accuracy of environmental impact estimation models over time.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose not described in this policy.

3. Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

Usage metrics and preferences are stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in chrome.storage API. This data is isolated to the Extension and is not accessible to other applications or websites.

Cloud Storage (Optional -Signed-In Users Only)

If you choose to sign in, your data is stored in Firebase Firestore, a managed Google Cloud service. All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS with TLS 1.3. Data at rest is protected using AES-256 encryption.

Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organisational safeguards to protect your information against unauthorised access, inadvertent disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Access to stored data is strictly limited to systems and personnel required to operate the Extension.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, license, or otherwise transfer your personal information to any third party for commercial purposes.

Data may be shared only in the following strictly limited circumstances:

Recipient Circumstance User Control
Firebase (Google Cloud) Storage for optional cross-device sync. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Opt-in only
Legal authorities Where required by applicable law, court order, or lawful governmental request. Not controllable
Rights protection Where reasonably necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of users or the public. Not controllable

5. Chrome Permissions

EcoQuery requests only the permissions strictly necessary to deliver its functionality, in accordance with the Chrome Web Store's minimum permissions requirement.

Permission Purpose
storage Saves usage metrics, goal settings, and preferences locally on your device across browser sessions.
alarms Schedules the daily reset of usage counters at midnight and, if enabled, triggers daily summary notifications.
notifications Delivers optional alerts when you approach your configured daily limit. Disabled by default; requires user activation.
host_permissions
chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com		 Detects query submission events on these two specific domains to estimate environmental impact. The Extension is active only on these URLs and no others.

6. Chrome Web Store Limited Use Policy Compliance

EcoQuery's collection and use of information obtained through Chrome APIs complies fully with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • Single purpose: User data is used solely to provide and improve EcoQuery's stated functionality -environmental impact tracking for ChatGPT usage.
  • Limited transfers: Data is transferred to third parties only where strictly necessary to deliver this functionality, to comply with applicable law, to protect against fraud or abuse, or as part of a corporate transaction with prior explicit user consent.
  • No unauthorised human access: Humans are prohibited from reading user data except where the user has given explicit consent, for security investigation, or to satisfy a legal obligation.
  • No prohibited uses: All other uses or transfers of user data -including advertising, credit assessment, data brokerage, or resale -are strictly prohibited.

In accordance with Limited Use disclosure requirements: "The use of information received from Chrome APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements."

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access: Your current usage metrics and settings are visible at any time through the Extension popup interface.
  • Deletion: Uninstalling the Extension permanently removes all locally stored data from your device. To request deletion of cloud account data, contact us using the details in Section 12. Account data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of your request.
  • Opt-out of notifications: Usage alerts may be disabled at any time through the Extension's settings panel without affecting core functionality.
  • Do Not Track: EcoQuery does not track users across third-party websites. Do Not Track signals are not applicable to this Extension's functionality.

8. Data Retention

  • Local data: Automatically and permanently removed upon uninstallation of the Extension.
  • Cloud data (account users): Retained for as long as the account remains active. Upon a deletion request, all associated data is permanently erased from Firebase within 30 days.
  • Inactive accounts: Accounts with no recorded activity for more than 24 consecutive months may be subject to deletion following advance notice. All associated data will be permanently removed.

9. Children's Privacy

EcoQuery is not directed at, nor intended for use by, individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the Extension, please contact us immediately. We will promptly investigate and delete any such information from our systems.

10. International Data Transfers

If you enable account-based synchronisation, your information may be transferred to and stored on servers located in the United States or other jurisdictions in which Google operates data centre infrastructure. Such transfers are conducted in accordance with applicable data protection law. By enabling synchronisation, you consent to the international transfer of your data as described herein.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, applicable law, or Extension functionality. In the event of a material change, the "Last updated" date at the top of this document will be revised. Your continued use of EcoQuery following publication of a revised policy constitutes your acceptance of the updated terms.
