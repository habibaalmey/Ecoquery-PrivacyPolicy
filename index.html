Privacy Policy

Effective: April 18, 2026 Applies to: Chrome Extension v1.3.0+
🔒

Our core commitment: EcoQuery does not collect, store, or transmit the content of your ChatGPT conversations. Only anonymised numerical metrics — such as estimated token counts and derived environmental calculations — are ever processed or retained.

EcoQuery ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data with transparency and care. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and disclose information when you use the EcoQuery Chrome Extension (the "Extension").

Please read this policy carefully before installing or using the Extension. By using EcoQuery, you acknowledge that you have read and understood the practices described herein. If you do not agree with these terms, please refrain from using the Extension.

01

Information We Collect

EcoQuery collects only the minimum information necessary to deliver its core functionality. The categories of data we process are as follows:

  • Usage Metrics: The number of ChatGPT queries submitted per day, estimated token counts, and derived environmental impact figures — including estimated CO₂ emissions, energy consumption (watt-hours), and water usage (millilitres).
  • User Preferences: Your selected daily usage goal (Easy, Medium, Hard, or Custom), notification preferences, and configured threshold settings.
  • Account Information (Optional): If you elect to create an account, we collect your email address and a unique user identifier solely to enable cross-device synchronisation of your metrics and preferences. Account creation is entirely optional.

At no point does EcoQuery access, read, store, or transmit the actual text content of your conversations with ChatGPT. The Extension operates exclusively on numerical signal data derived from interaction events.

02

How We Use Your Information

All information collected by EcoQuery is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Calculating and displaying real-time environmental impact metrics (CO₂, energy, and water estimates) within the extension interface.
  • Tracking your progress toward self-defined daily usage goals and providing usage summaries.
  • Persisting your preferences and settings across browser sessions using Chrome's local storage API.
  • Delivering optional notifications when you approach or exceed your chosen usage threshold.
  • Enabling cross-device synchronisation of your data (only when you are signed in to an account).
  • Improving the accuracy of our environmental impact estimation models over time.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the functionality described above.

03

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage: Usage metrics and preferences are stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in chrome.storage API. This data is isolated to the Extension and is not accessible to other applications or websites.

Cloud Storage (Optional — Account Users Only): If you choose to sign in, your data is stored in Firebase Firestore, a managed Google Cloud service. All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS with TLS 1.3. Data at rest is protected using AES-256 encryption.

Security Measures: We implement appropriate technical and organisational safeguards to protect your information against unauthorised access, inadvertent disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Access to stored data is strictly limited to systems and personnel required to operate the Extension.

04

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, license, or otherwise transfer your personal information to any third party for commercial purposes.

Your data may be shared only in the following strictly limited circumstances:

  • Firebase (Google Cloud): When you opt in to account-based synchronisation, your data is stored within Firebase infrastructure. This service is operated by Google and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Legal Obligations: We may disclose information if required to do so by applicable law, court order, or in response to a lawful request from a governmental authority.
  • Protection of Rights: We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of EcoQuery, our users, or the public.
05

Chrome Permissions

EcoQuery requests the following Chrome permissions. Each is limited to a specific, necessary function and is not used beyond the purpose stated:

  • storage: Saves your usage metrics, goal settings, and preferences locally on your device between browser sessions.
  • alarms: Schedules the daily reset of usage counters at midnight and, if enabled, triggers daily summary notifications.
  • notifications: Delivers optional alerts when you approach your configured daily usage limit. This feature is opt-in and can be disabled at any time.
  • host_permissions (chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com): Enables the Extension to detect query events on these specific domains for the purpose of estimating environmental impact. The Extension is active only on these two URLs and operates on no other websites.
06

Chrome Web Store Limited Use Policy

EcoQuery's collection and use of information obtained through Chrome APIs complies fully with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • User data is used solely to provide and improve EcoQuery's stated single purpose: environmental impact tracking for ChatGPT usage.
  • User data is transferred to third parties only where strictly necessary to deliver or improve this functionality, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a corporate transaction with prior user consent.
  • Human access to user data is prohibited except in limited, documented circumstances: where the user has given explicit consent, for security investigation, or to satisfy a legal obligation.
  • All other uses or transfers of user data — including but not limited to advertising, credit assessment, or data brokerage — are strictly prohibited.
07

Your Rights and Choices

You retain the following rights with respect to your data:

  • Access: Your current usage metrics and settings are visible at any time via the Extension popup interface.
  • Deletion: Uninstalling the Extension permanently removes all locally stored data from your device. To delete cloud-based account data, please contact us using the details in Section 12. Account data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of your request.
  • Opt-Out of Notifications: You may disable usage alerts at any time through the Extension's settings panel.
  • Do Not Track: EcoQuery does not track users across third-party websites and does not respond to Do Not Track browser signals, as such tracking is outside the scope of this Extension's functionality.
08

Data Retention

Local Data: All data stored locally on your device is automatically and permanently removed upon uninstallation of the Extension.

Cloud Data (Account Users): Data associated with a signed-in account is retained for as long as the account remains active. Upon account deletion, all associated data will be permanently erased from Firebase within 30 days.

Inactive Accounts: Accounts that have not recorded any activity for a period exceeding 24 consecutive months may be subject to deletion. Affected users will be notified prior to any deletion, and all associated data will be permanently removed.

09

Children's Privacy

EcoQuery is not directed at, nor intended for use by, individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or legal guardian and have reason to believe that your child has provided personal information through the Extension, please contact us immediately. We will take prompt steps to investigate and delete any such information from our systems.

10

International Data Transfers

If you choose to enable account-based synchronisation, your information may be transferred to and stored on servers located in the United States or other jurisdictions in which Google operates data centre infrastructure. Such transfers are carried out in accordance with applicable data protection laws. By enabling synchronisation, you consent to the international transfer of your data as described in this section.
